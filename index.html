FAQs

Questions travel agencies ask.

Understand how we work before taking the next step.

Do you work specifically with travel agencies?

DigiXperts Hub focuses on performance marketing for UAE travel agencies and tourism businesses.

Which advertising platforms do you manage?

Our services include Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram, Google Ads, retargeting and campaign optimization.

Can you guarantee a specific number of bookings?

No. Results depend on factors such as your offer, audience, budget, competition and sales process. We focus on measurement and ongoing improvement rather than guaranteed outcomes.

Is the advertising budget included in your service fee?

Advertising spend is generally separate from management fees and is paid to the advertising platform by the client.

How much should my agency spend on ads?

The right budget depends on your goals, market, offer and testing needs. We recommend discussing your situation before deciding.

Can you help if we receive leads but few bookings?

We can review campaign targeting, lead quality, tracking and the inquiry journey to identify possible areas for improvement.

How do you measure campaign performance?

Depending on available tracking and data, we can assess metrics such as cost per lead, lead quality and conversion actions. Booking and revenue tracking may require additional setup.
